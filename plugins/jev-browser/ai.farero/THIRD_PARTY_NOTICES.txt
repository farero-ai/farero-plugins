Jev Browser for Farero contains material from jev-ultrafast by Browser Use:
https://github.com/browser-use/jev-ultrafast, commit cf3c406f0fc4becff8986ed4d4d37b7b9f102e45.

- ai.farero/snapshot.js is jev_ultrafast/snapshot.js, copied verbatim below a copy of this notice.
- ai.farero/run.ts contains the NEXT_ACTION and TARGET prompts from jev_ultrafast/questions.py and
  the in-page settle and hit-test scripts from jev_ultrafast/browser.py, copied verbatim. Its action
  loop is a port of jev_ultrafast/agent.py, model.py and browser.py to TypeScript.
